## 🔴 1. Integrity bugs fixed (the dangerous ones) / رفع باگ‌های اعتباری

### 1.1 Fake promo codes removed from `/deals` — کدهای تخفیف ساختگی حذف شدند
The deals page advertised "exclusive" codes **CREATORAI20, DESCRIPT15, CREATOR50, SUB10**
marked "Verified / Tested and active" — none of them exist or were agreed with any vendor.
This was the same class of deception the earlier audit removed from affiliate links.
- The page now lists only what is verifiable: genuinely free tools, freemium tiers, free
  trials and **source-checked prices with the vendor link and check date**.
- Vendors can submit a real deal (we test it at checkout before publishing).
- An E2E test asserts the fake codes never come back.

### 1.2 "(Verified Test)" labels could no longer fall back to seed ratings
`blog/[slug]` and `CompareClient` had `tool.scores ? … : tool.rating` fallbacks that would
label an unverified seed rating as a verified test. Now gated strictly on `hasVerifiedScore`.
Also removed a hardcoded `lastReviewed: 'Aug 2026'` fallback in the compare grid.

### 1.3 Public API no longer leaks seed ratings
`/api/v1/tools` returned the unverified seed rating as `rating` and as `verified_score`
fallback. `verified_score` is now strictly null unless hands-on-tested, the misleading
`rating`/`popularity_index` fields are removed, `pricing_source_url/checked_at` added,
and hardcoded `creatorsaicenter.vercel.app` URLs replaced with `SITE_URL`
(the audit-1.10 single-source-of-truth had been missed in this file). `/developers`
docs updated to match — including a sample that honestly shows a pricing-verified tool.

### 1.4 Fabricated testimonials deleted
`src/data/spotlights.ts` (unused "creator spotlights" with invented quotes from
"The Infographics Lab") was orphaned fake social proof. Deleted entirely.

### 1.5 Blog content scrubbed of invented tests, surveys and numbers
Eight hand-written posts were reviewed line-by-line. Fabrications removed/rewritten:
- "We spent two weeks testing… 96% caption accuracy" (Top-5 Shorts post) → honest
  catalog-based guide stating we have NOT tested them yet.
- "We benchmarked 4 models, scored 240 outputs blind" (Sora vs Veo vs Kling vs Runway)
  → rewritten as documented-capabilities comparison; title no longer claims a test.
- "In our blind listening test, 8/10 listeners…" (ElevenLabs vs HeyGen) → removed.
- "Channels reach monetization in 3-6 months on average… $4,000-12,000/month"
  (Faceless post) → honest "costs predictable, revenue uncertain" framing; retitled.
- "Our 40-video sample averaged 23% higher CTR" / "12%+ CTR" (Midjourney post) → removed.
- "An education channel we interviewed: +31% watch time, +$1,900/mo" (Dubbing post) →
  removed; title no longer promises "Double Your AdSense".
- "every podcast we surveyed in 2026" → removed.

### 1.6 Auto-blog gets a real editorial gate (Helpful-Content defence)
- `BlogPost.editoriallyReviewed?: boolean` added. `scripts/generate-blog.mjs` now writes
  `editoriallyReviewed: false` on every machine-drafted post.
- Unreviewed posts are **noindexed** and show a visible "AI-assisted draft" label on the
  post page and the blog index. Hand-written posts are treated as reviewed.
- The flag is documented in code: never flip it automatically.
